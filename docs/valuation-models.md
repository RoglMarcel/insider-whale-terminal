# Scrapling and fair value

GitHub Actions opts into Scrapling 0.4.15 for the existing public OpenInsider and SECForm4 tables. The TypeScript column mappers, trade sanitization, persistence, scoring and portfolio rules remain unchanged. Other existing integrations keep their original transports. The Python fetcher is bounded by host allowlists, request timeouts, process limits and response size, with no credentials, CAPTCHA solving or proxy rotation.

`fundamentals.py` collects public Stockrow annual statements and observable GuruFocus earnings/growth. Exact Macrotrends statement URLs can be provided in the repository variable `MACROTRENDS_URLS`, a JSON map from ticker to HTTPS URL. Macrotrends can deny public requests; a rejected response never becomes a financial fact. TIKR's application requires an account and no documented public financial feed has been verified. There is **no claim that all four providers can be scraped without access**.

The first GitHub runner returned HTTP 403 for both Stockrow and GuruFocus, despite successful prior public fetches in the development environment. `data/valuation-baseline.json` preserves those actual AAPL observations from October 3, 2026 as an initial dated dataset. It is not synthetic test data or a live connection. The publisher keeps this dataset when collection returns no usable data and prefers newer verified provider observations when available. The public panel preserves each original retrieval, statement and price date; no failed refresh is presented as fresh finance data. Other tickers remain unavailable until their inputs can be collected or supplied through an authorized export.

## Data contract

A provider dataset contains ticker, provider (`stockrow`, `gurufocus`, `tikr`, `macrotrends`), source URL, fetchedAt, currency, statementDate, facts and optional annual histories, industry peers and segments. No providers are silently merged. The panel chooses one provider and attributes all calculations to it.

Amounts are full currency units, rates are fractions, share counts are actual shares, and EPS/dividends are per share. Every fact carries its period and unit. Missing/unknown values are absent, never zero. Stockrow's month-only fiscal dates retain month precision. Its explicit million-dollar scaling is checked before parsing. A cash-flow working-capital contribution is negated to obtain investment in working capital. Effective tax rate, dividends per share and capital are derived from same-period facts and marked as derived. Equal total and common book equity identifies zero non-common *book* claims; this is not a valuation of unreported off-balance-sheet claims. Stockrow's inconsistent `P/Operating CF` row is deliberately unused. GuruFocus's observable TTM EPS and growth excluding non-recurring items retain their distinct bases.

`publish-valuations.ts` validates all fields and emits only allowlisted data into `public/data/valuations.json`. It drops arbitrary extra fields, invalid units, incorrect identity/currency and credential-bearing or non-provider URLs. Provider errors never overwrite good cached records. Annual inputs refresh with a seven-day cache; a four-minute collection budget rotates through uncached tickers over scheduled runs. The UI states plainly when a ticker has not yet obtained sufficient data. Prices retain their observed dates; upside comparisons require a price no older than ten days.

## Models and assumptions

All 26 requested methods are implemented separately in `src/lib/valuation/calculate.ts`. Available values, missing inputs, inapplicable assumptions and stale financial dates are distinct states. There is no median across correlated methods and no inferred industry peer multiple. Historical multiples use up to ten distinct positive annual observations, with a minimum of three. CAPE requires ten inflation-adjusted EPS observations, not ten nominal earnings figures. Industry P/E requires at least three identified same-industry peers excluding the target company.

Defaults are **scenarios**, not estimates of observed capital costs: five years, 3% projection growth, 2% perpetual growth, 10% WACC/equity/unlevered rates, 4% comparison yield and target PEG 1. Users can edit/reset them. Negative cash flow, missing debt/shares, inconsistent periods, missing specialist assumptions, nonpositive final equity and discount rates at or below perpetual growth prevent the affected model from displaying a fair value. DCF discounts FCFF at WACC and FCFE at cost of equity; enterprise models subtract debt and non-common claims once and add cash once. Residual income evolves book equity using retained earnings, then explicitly assumes perpetual growth of next-period residual income; EVA is a constant-ROIC scenario. The Stuttgart method is labeled historical, not presented as a current tax or exchange valuation standard. Real options require project value, investment, project volatility, option term and a base equity value excluding the option. SOTP requires actual segment EBIT, segment-specific multiples and central-cost present value.

APV tax shields/distress costs, liquidation costs, replacement asset values, adjusted Stuttgart earnings, inflation-adjusted histories and segment/project estimates cannot be derived honestly from a generic public statement. These models remain unavailable until those specific inputs are supplied. Optional normalized provider exports can be placed on the runner in `tmp/fundamentals-imports/<provider>/<TICKER>.json` before collection. Only the validated financial output is public; raw account exports, tokens and cookies must never be committed to this public repository. An authorized export is not advertised as a live provider connection.

The feature does **not** populate `TickerAggregate.upsidePct` or rescore recorded signals. Historical confidence, scoring, cash-drag calculations and portfolio fills stay unchanged. The new valuation panel is informational and recalculates its own methods when assumptions change.

Primary methodology references: [Damodaran, valuation](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/lectures/val.html), [APV](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/apv.htm), [EVA](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/invfables/eva.htm). Verification includes mathematical reference cases, all 26 complete-input models, missing/negative/stale/mixed-period cases, normalization and credential stripping, plus desktop/tablet/mobile browser scenarios.
